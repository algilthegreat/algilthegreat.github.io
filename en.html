Two cultures. Two cities. One programme.

Two cultures. Two cities. One programme. 

Celebrating music, dance, cinema, design, literature, gastronomymusic

For nineteen days, the France–Laos Festival links Luang Prabang and Vientiane through contemporary creation and living heritage. Music, dance, film, visual arts, literature, design, gastronomy: a river of encounters between French and Lao artists.

The festival

See the programme

Luang Prabang opens the festival from 3 to 9 November, then the festival travels down the Mekong to Vientiane, from 10 to 21 November.
