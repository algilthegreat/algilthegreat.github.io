Two cultures. Two cities. One programme.

Two cultures. Two cities. One programme. 

Celebrating music, dance, cinema, visual arts, literature, digital artsmusic

For nineteen days, the France–Laos Festival links Luang Prabang and Vientiane through contemporary creation and living heritage. Music, dance, film, visual arts, literature, digital arts: a river of encounters between French and Lao artists.

The festival

The festival opens in Luang Prabang on 3 November and continues there until 21 November; in Vientiane it runs from 10 to 21 November.
