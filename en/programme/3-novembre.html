3 November 2026 · Luang Prabang

Festival opening night

The France–Laos Festival 2026 opens on 3 November at the Institut français in Luang Prabang, with Véronique de Lavenère and the opening of Charlotte Andraca Tosseri’s exhibition in the garden. A free evening, open to all.

  • Institut français du Laos — Luang Prabang
  • 18:30 – 19:30
  • Free entry
