3 November 2026 · Luang Prabang

Festival opening night

The France–Laos Festival 2026 opens at sunset on the Mekong riverbank. A free evening, open to all.

  • Mekong Riverside
  • 18:00 – 22:30
  • Free entry

Evening programme

  1. 18:00Welcome and traditional baci ceremony
  2. 18:30Official speeches
  3. 19:00Dance solo — Noy Phetsavanh
  4. 19:30“Mekong Lights” projection — Thomas Girard
  5. 20:00“Mekong Suite” premiere
  6. 21:30Reception and DJ set by Collectif Mékong Sound

Speeches

Official addresses by representatives of the Institut français du Laos, Lao authorities and festival partners.

Reception

The evening continues with a reception open to all and a riverside DJ set.
