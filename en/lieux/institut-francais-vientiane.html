Institut français du Laos — Vientiane

About

Headquarters of the Institut français du Laos: theatre, gallery, media library and language centre. It hosts concerts, professional meetings and workshops throughout the second half of the festival.

Practical info

Address
Avenue Lane Xang, Vientiane — Vientiane
Opening hours
Mon–Sat 9am–6pm, and on event evenings.
Getting there
City centre, close to Patuxai.
Accessibility
Step-free access, assistance on request.
