Institut français du Laos — Luang Prabang

About

The Institut français branch in Luang Prabang offers language courses, a media library and cultural events all year round. During the festival it becomes the beating heart of the opening week.

Practical info

Address
Centre-ville, Luang Prabang — Luang Prabang
Opening hours
Mon–Sat 9am–6pm, and on event evenings.
Getting there
Walking distance from the old town; tuk-tuk or bicycle.
Accessibility
Step-free access, assistance on request.
