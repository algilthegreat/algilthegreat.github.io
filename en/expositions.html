  • 04 NOV → 28 NOV

    URAMADO AR, the Awakening of the Tanukis

    Uramado AR is an augmented reality trail by Julie Stephen Chheng that tells the story of the Tanukis, forest spirits awakening in the city. Up to 48 animals are placed around the venue, indoors and outdoors, more or less hidden and in a wide range of sizes. Visitors are invited to download the free app URAMADO AR, the Awakening of the Tanukis (on iOS and Android) to begin the treasure hunt. As they are discovered, the spirits come to life, wonder and question the visitor. Depending on the answers, visitors may find out that they are a spirit themselves, and which Totem Animal they are. They can then leave with an augmented reality mask and/or paper toy. Uramado poetically invites the public to question our differences, weaving a narrative where reality blends with imagination and unfolds endlessly. Workshops, a live VJ set and an Uramado book accompany the trail, so the public can experience the Uramado world in several dimensions. In Luang Prabang, the trail is set up outdoors at the Institut français. Extended after the festival, until 28 November.

  • 10 NOV → 28 NOV

    Fortune Teller

    Fortune Teller is an interactive projection by Julie Stephen Chheng in which visitors come face to face with Spirits of Nature. They spin past like a slot machine until the visitor, stepping on a floor tile, triggers one of them: each encounter gives rise to a message to interpret and a dreamlike setting. Echoing childhood games and card games, the work invites everyone to connect their own story with the message they receive. This is not about fortune-telling but about tracing a path between the symbols of the elements, inspired by the Book of Changes (I Ching) and its eight trigrams. Gift cards featuring the eight spirits (fire, water, earth, thunder, sky, wood, light and shadow) are handed out to visitors. With the free Fortune Teller AR app, they come to life and share their philosophies of the world. Created and directed by Julie Stephen Chheng · Animation: Thomas Pons · Development: Julien Hognon (Volumique) · Music: Apollo Noir · Touch development: Pauline Millet.
